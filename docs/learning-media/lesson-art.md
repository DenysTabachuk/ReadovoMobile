## Exact prompts

### Reading check callout (transparent background)

Create a small standalone decorative illustration for a reading comprehension callout in an English learning app. Two overlapping tactile painterly paper speech bubbles: a warm cream front bubble with three simple dark dots, and a muted turquoise rear bubble. A few golden autumn leaves tucked around the bottom-left. Charming polished editorial storybook style, soft subtle dimensional shading, warm rich materials, compact square composition, isolated on a fully transparent background, generous transparent margin. No text, no letters, no words, no UI, no frame or badge, no rectangular background, no watermark. Must remain crisp and recognizable when displayed at 72x80 logical pixels on a dark charcoal app card.

### Reading

Original editorial artwork ONLY, no user interface, no text, no lettering, no watermark. Wide panoramic 2.2:1 landscape illustration for an English-learning mobile app. Warm richly detailed painterly realistic storybook travel illustration, soft honey autumn sunshine, natural materials, attractive Munich/Bavarian festival scene, blue-white bunting, rich restrained colours. Two adult friends Emma and Ben visiting a friendly Oktoberfest pretzel stall, Ben wearing a green Bavarian hat, Emma holding a warm pretzel, friendly joyful faces, sunlit festival tents and Munich architecture softly behind them. Compose them on either side leaving quiet centre breathing space, warm autumn foliage. No beer or alcohol.

### Comprehension

Original editorial artwork ONLY, no user interface, no text, no lettering, no watermark. Wide panoramic 2.2:1 landscape illustration for an English-learning mobile app. Warm richly detailed painterly realistic storybook travel illustration, soft honey autumn sunshine, natural materials, attractive Munich/Bavarian festival scene, blue-white bunting, rich restrained colours. Neutral decorative atmosphere for comprehension questions about a fictional Oktoberfest visit: close foreground autumn orange leaves and warm paper lanterns, tasteful blue-white bunting over a charming quiet Bavarian courtyard, softly blurred half-timbered facades, gentle golden afternoon light. No people, no transport, no station, no hats, no clothing, no food, no instruments or dancing. Must not visually reveal answers about travel, hats, food, music or emotions. Rich tactile painterly details, peaceful balanced wide composition.
